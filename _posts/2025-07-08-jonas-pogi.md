For the past four-and-a-half years, there have been two riders who could contest the Tour de France: Tadej Pogacar, and Jonas Vingegaard. It is already one of cycling's historic rivalries. To show just how close it has been, I decided to have a look at the _overall_ time difference between the pair:

![img](/img/cycling/jonas_pogi.png)

This graph shows the time difference, in seconds, between them, with a positive delta being in Pogacar's favour and a negative delta in Vingegaard's, such that

$\delta = t_{pogi} - t_{jonas}$

In addition, I show with (dashed) or without (solid) bonification seconds (won by placing in the top-3 of individual stages).

This graph then neatly tells the story of 4.5 years of rivalry. It shows Pogacar reigning supreme in 2021, after dramatically snatching the 2020 victory. A yearlong uncontested reign seemed inevitable. But then, in 2022, Jonas surprised everyone, in particular with the historic two-pronged attack on Granon. He continued this into 2023, most notably on Col de la Loze ("I'm gone, I'm dead").
But Pogacar clawed his way back, to reign supreme once again in 2024 and, from the looks of it, will continue to do so in 2025.

And so, after 4.5 years, the balance between them is incredibly close to $0$ seconds!

Some additional remarks:
Pogacar gains time _more often_, in particular with boni-sprints as well, and wins (way) more stages. Vingegaard, however, brings the hammer down _hard_ when he gains time - the average gap is much larger.

Vingegaard and Pogacar have shared between them $75\%$ of the yellow jerseys in this time period.

Let's see what the future has in store.
